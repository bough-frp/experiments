walk?: trials where the walk design found something other than its expected
result (a cycle at tx1 relink, or nothing). grey+k: found by the grey mark k
transactions after the move. never: not found in 20. no input in: of those,
no input reaches the cycle at all. grey=reach: the grey mark fired exactly at the
first transaction whose input reaches the switch.

Verdict: of 15 illegal cases, the walk is what finds 9; in the grey design the grey mark finds 3 of those, all transactions later than the walk (F50 10,000 upstream, move closes cycle; R10b move closes cycle, no reader; same, output merged with input y), 5 are found by another guard (R10b, then Runtime::sample; R10b, cell listener on the loop; first link closes cycle, construct; first link closes cycle, build; switch_stream own output, build), and 1 never (switch_stream selects own output). 3 of the 9 are found at the same transaction and phase. The other 6 are found by Brent before either check runs, in both designs, and overflow without it.
