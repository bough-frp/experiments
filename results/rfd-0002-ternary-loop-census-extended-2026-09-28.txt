== Reachable-state mode ==

Same programs. Each gate cell is now `c = src.map(pred).hold(init)`, src an
input or any stream of the program, the loop's own included (the gate reads c
before the instant, so that is no dependency), pred one of the four filter
predicates, init false or true, drawn once per program from a second seed.
Only cell states reachable from the initial one, over instants the input
model allows, are checked: breadth-first over the at most 4 states.
free: constructive over free cells (as above). reach: over reachable states.
new: constructive over reachable states but not over free cells, split into
(a'd)  dead gate: some gate is closed in every reachable state; with those
       gates deleted, the program is (a) over free cells.
(a'i)  invariant: acyclic in every reachable state once closed gates are cut,
       not by dead gates alone. A switch on the same cells expresses it.
(c1), (c2): as (b1), (b2), judged over the reachable states.
val: of (c), constructive only because of filter predicates on values.
fix: of (c), every gate on or upstream of a cycle is open in every reachable
     state or closed in every one: its cells are constants, and the cycle is a
     (b) pattern.
