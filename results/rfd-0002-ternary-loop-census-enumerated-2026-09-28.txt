ternary-loop-census --enumerate: every program of 2 to 4 nodes, every cell binding

Every program of 2 to 4 nodes over the reachable-state mode's subset that has a
same-instant cycle, counted once per orbit under renaming nodes, permuting
inputs, swapping the two cells and negating a cell. Each cell a gate reads is
`c = src.map(pred).hold(init)`, and every binding is tried: src any input or
stream, pred one of four, init false or true, per cell. Classes as in the
reachable-state mode.
free: constructive over free cells. mixed: some cell states settle in every
instant and some don't; only these can be new. new: some binding makes it
constructive over reachable states. (a'd) (a'i) (c1) (c2): some binding of
that class. chg: some (c) binding with a changing cell: a gate on or upstream
of a cycle is open in one reachable state and closed in another. val: some (c)
binding that is value-dependent. bindings, new-b, (c)-b, chg-b: (program, binding) pairs
over the mixed programs.
